02  /  How it works

Four stones, placed in order.

Every step makes the browser safer. Scroll and the cairn grows, one stone per step.

0 / 4 stones
01 / BROWSE

A browser that is on your side.

Tabs, private tabs, bookmarks and a smart address bar. The shield and downloads sit right in the top bar.

  • Private tabs keep nothing in history
  • No account, nothing to sign up for
02 / BLOCK

Ads and trackers, gone.

Open filter lists stop ads and trackers before the page loads. YouTube plays without ad breaks, full screen in portrait or landscape.

  • uBlock Origin, EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists
  • Per-site shield in the address bar
  • YouTube with no ads
03 / DOWNLOAD

Eight parts, one fast file.

Each file is split into up to 8 parts and downloaded together. Here a 310 MB video downloads at 27 MB/s. Pause and resume any time.

  • Up to 8 parts at once
  • Pause and resume without starting over
  • Files sorted into folders by type
04 / FEEL SAFE

Read in peace, stay private.

Reader mode shows just the article. HTTPS upgrade, cookie controls and tracking-tag removal keep you private.

  • Reader mode with text size controls
  • HTTPS upgrade and cookie controls
  • Strips tags like utm_source and fbclid
