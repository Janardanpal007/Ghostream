Highlights

Everything a browser should be.

Free and open source, with no account. Privacy and speed are built in, not added on.

◎

Free & open source

GPL-3.0 licensed. Read the code, build it yourself.

⊘

Built-in ad blocker

Blocks ads and trackers with open filter lists, per site if you want.

↓

Video downloads

Pick the quality and save videos from supported sites.

⌁

Torrents

Open magnet links and .torrent files right in the app.

◉

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your history stays on your phone.

▦

Organized folders

Downloads sort into Videos, Music, Documents and more.

Browser

Fast, clean and yours.

Tabs, private mode, bookmarks and a smart address bar, with reader mode for distraction-free reading.

⧉

Tabs and private mode

Open many tabs, or go private so nothing is kept in history.

☆

Bookmarks and history

Save pages and find them again. Clear history any time.

⌕

Smart address bar

Type a site, a search or a link. Ghostream figures out which.

≡

Reader mode

Just the article, in clean text, with text size controls.

Download engine

8 parts. Pause anytime.

Each file is split into up to 8 parts and downloaded at once. Pause, resume, and keep downloading in the background.

⇣

Background downloads

Keep browsing while files download. Progress shows in the notification bar.

▤

Organized folders

Every file goes to a folder by type, so your phone stays tidy.

Video

Watch and save, your way.

Choose the quality, watch full screen in portrait or landscape, and control speed and picture-in-picture.

Privacy & protection

Feel safe while you browse.

Ads and trackers are blocked. Connections use HTTPS when they can, and you decide what cookies to keep.

⛨

Ad & tracker blocking

Open filter lists stop ads and trackers before they load.

🔒

HTTPS upgrade

Upgrades connections to HTTPS when the site supports it.

🍪

Cookie controls

Allow all, block third-party, or block all cookies.

✂

Tracking-tag removal

Strips tags like utm_source and fbclid from links.

⇄

Optional proxy

Route traffic through an HTTP or SOCKS5 proxy if you use one.
