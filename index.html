Block

Shields up. Ads gone.

Ghostream blocks ads and trackers with open filter lists. Turn the shield off for one site if you need to.

  • Block trackers & ads with uBlock Origin, EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists.
  • Per-site shield in the address bar.
  • Cookie control: all, no third-party, or none.

Download

8 parts. One fast file.

Most browsers download a file in one piece. Ghostream splits it into up to 8 parts and downloads them together.

  • Up to 8 parts at once for faster downloads.
  • Pause and resume without starting over.
  • Background downloads with progress in the notification bar.
